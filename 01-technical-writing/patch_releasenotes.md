## Patch Release Notes Management

### Responsibilities

- **Secondary Review:** Performed a secondary review of patch release notes after approval by the Engineering Lead to verify content accuracy, consistency, and completeness.
- **Jira Ticket Status Management:** Updated Jira ticket statuses to reflect the latest information and progress relevant to the patch release documentation process.
- **Upgrade Considerations:** Gathered upgrade-related information from multiple teams, consolidated the inputs, and updated the Upgrade Considerations section to communicate relevant changes and requirements.
- **Resolved Issues:** Documented verified defect fixes included in the patch release based on information provided by engineering and other relevant teams.
- **Known Issues:** Updated the Known Issues section with confirmed information about outstanding issues and their impact.
- **Patch Contents:** Added and maintained the Patch Contents section to provide a consolidated view of the changes and fixes included in the release.

### Release Documentation Workflow

1. Collect the release-related information from engineering and other relevant teams.
2. Updated the applicable sections of the patch release notes.
3. Consolidated upgrade considerations, resolved issues, known issues, and patch contents.
4. Updated the relevant Jira ticket statuses.
5. Performed a secondary review after the Engineering Lead approved the release notes.
6. Checked the final document for accuracy, consistency, and completeness.
